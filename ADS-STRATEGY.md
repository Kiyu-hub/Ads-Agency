## Executive Summary

Clasikal Homes sells luxury home decor and furnishings to design-conscious Ghanaians who want homes that reflect their personal taste and status. The brand voice is aspirational, emotional, and craft-focused — "every piece tells a story." The dominant visual identity is royal blue (#2345C8) against warm, editorial interior photography.

The strategy is built around three truths about the Ghanaian luxury decor buyer:
1. **Discovery happens on social** — Facebook and Instagram are where aspirational purchases begin in Ghana.
2. **Trust precedes purchase** — luxury items require brand familiarity before a Ghanaian buyer converts; retargeting is critical.
3. **Transformation, not product** — the emotional hook is "what your home becomes," not "what the product looks like."

## Platform Strategy

### Recommended Mix

| Platform | Role | Budget % | Rationale |
|----------|------|----------|-----------|
| **Meta (Facebook + Instagram)** | Primary | 60% | Dominant social platform in Ghana; home decor performs exceptionally on IG; ASC campaigns enable broad discovery |
| **Google (Shopping + Search)** | Secondary | 30% | Captures high-intent "home decor Ghana", "luxury furniture Accra" searches; product feed drives Shopping |
| **TikTok** | Testing | 10% | Growing fast in Ghana 18–35 demo; room transformation content highly shareable |

> **Note:** LinkedIn and Microsoft Ads are not recommended — wrong audience for consumer home decor in Ghana.

### Why Ghana Changes the Math
- Meta CPMs in Ghana run **$1–3** (vs. $15–30 in the US) — exceptional reach per cedi spent.
- Google CPCs for home-related terms run **$0.10–0.50** locally.
- This means a $500/month budget achieves what would cost $3,000+ in the US.
- **WhatsApp** (not an ad platform) should be the primary post-click conversion tool — include a WhatsApp CTA on all landing pages.

## Messaging Framework

### Core Emotional Hook
> "Your home is a reflection of who you are. Clasikal Homes gives you the pieces to make it extraordinary."

### Three Content Pillars

| Pillar | Message | Formats |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Transformation** | Before/after, room makeovers, styling reveals | Reels, TikTok, Carousel |
| **Craftsmanship** | Close-up product details, quality stories, founder story | Static images, Stories |
| **Aspiration** | Lifestyle interiors, "dream home" framing | Static, Collection ads |

### Tone Guidelines (from Brand DNA)
- Lead with emotion, not specs
- Use "your home", "your space", "your story" — make it personal
- Short punchy copy — "Elevate every corner." not a paragraph
- Never use generic stock-photo language ("family smiling in a kitchen")
- Royal blue (#2345C8) must appear in all ad creative

## Competitive Positioning

Ghana luxury home decor space is fragmented. Most competitors rely on organic Instagram with low ad investment. Paid advertising creates a significant first-mover advantage. Key differentiators to emphasize in ads:
- **Curated selection** (not generic mass-market)
- **Transformative aesthetic** (emotional, not transactional)
- **Local brand** (founded by Alhaji Latif Sania — trust signal for Ghanaian buyers)
