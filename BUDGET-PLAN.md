# Budget Plan — Clasikal Homes

## Budget Tiers

Choose based on current monthly ad capacity:

| Tier | Monthly Budget | Best For |
|------|----------------|----------|
| **Starter** | $300–$600 | Testing messaging, building pixel data |
| **Growth** | $600–$1,500 | Scaling winners, adding Google |
| **Scale** | $1,500–$3,000+ | Full platform mix, aggressive growth |

> **Ghana advantage:** CPMs run $1–3 (vs $15–30 in Western markets). A $500 budget in Ghana delivers the equivalent reach of ~$3,000 in the US.

---

## Starter Tier ($300–$600/month)

| Platform | Budget | Campaigns |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Meta | $300–$500 (100%) | ASC only — let Meta optimize |
| Google | $0 | Not yet — insufficient data |
| TikTok | $0 | Not yet |

**Focus:** Feed the pixel. Run 1 ASC campaign with 5–8 creatives. Goal is 20+ purchases/month to unlock Smart Bidding.

## 70/20/10 Allocation (ongoing)

```
70% → Proven performers (campaigns with ROAS ≥ 3.0)
20% → Scaling (campaigns with ROAS 2.0–3.0, showing upward trend)
10% → Testing (new creatives, audiences, platforms)
```

| Month | Phase | Action |

## Scaling Rules

### Scale Up (20% Rule)
- Actual CPA < target CPA by >10%
- Campaign NOT in learning phase (Meta: 50 events/week minimum)
- Increase budget by max 20% at once
- Wait 5–7 days before next increase

### Kill (3× Rule)
- Spend > 3× target CPA with 0 conversions
- Pause immediately — don't restart without creative/targeting change

### Warning Signs
- Meta frequency > 4.0 (7-day) → refresh creative
- Google Impression Share > 80% → raise bids or expand keywords
- ROAS declining 2 weeks in a row → audit creative fatigue first
