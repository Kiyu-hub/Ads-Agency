# Creative Brief — Clasikal Homes

**Brand:** Clasikal Homes
**Market:** Ghana
**Primary Color:** #2345C8 (Royal Blue)
**Voice:** Aspirational · Warm · Elegant · Craft-focused
**Core Message:** "A home reflecting modern and unique decor."

---

## Creative Direction

### Visual Rules
- Always feature real interior spaces — no plain white product-only backgrounds
- Royal blue (#2345C8) must appear in every ad (overlay, text box, logo badge, or frame)
- Warm, editorial lighting — golden hour tones, soft shadows, layered textures
- Clean typography — large, white or cream headline text over lifestyle imagery
- Never use generic smiling-family stock photos

### Copy Rules
- Lead with transformation: "Your corner. Elevated." not "Buy our sofa."
- Use "your" frequently — make it personal
- Short headlines (≤6 words), longer body copy optional for Stories/Facebook
- End with a clear CTA: "Shop the Collection" / "Explore Now" / "Chat with Us on WhatsApp"

## Format Spec Summary

| Platform | Format | Dimensions | Safe Zone |
|----------|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Meta Feed | Static image | 1080×1080 (1:1) | Keep text out of bottom 14% |
| Meta Feed | Landscape | 1200×628 (1.91:1) | Standard |
| Meta Stories/Reels | Video | 1080×1920 (9:16) | Keep key content in center 80% |
| Meta Carousel | Each card | 1080×1080 | Text overlay max 20% of image |
| Google PMax | Image (landscape) | 1200×628 | No text required (Google adds it) |
| Google PMax | Image (square) | 1200×1200 | No text required |
| TikTok | Video | 1080×1920 (9:16) | Avoid bottom 20% (UI overlay) |
| Google RSA | Text only | Headline: 30 chars, Description: 90 chars | N/A |
