## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Clasikal Homes enters Ghana's $409M home furnishings market at the ideal moment: the luxury segment is growing at 4.39% annually, competitors are uniformly unsophisticated in paid advertising, and Ghana's digital infrastructure (mobile penetration, Meta reach, WhatsApp commerce) creates a compounding acquisition advantage for a digital-first brand.

**The core opportunity:** No competitor in Ghana's luxury home decor space runs structured Meta catalog ads, Google Shopping campaigns, or consistent TikTok content. The entire digital acquisition infrastructure — retargeting, lookalike audiences, dynamic product ads — is open for Clasikal to build and own before competitors realize the threat.

**The core risk:** WhatsApp is the primary conversion path in Ghana, not website checkout. Every paid traffic strategy that ignores this burns budget. Fix the attribution gap between ad click and WhatsApp conversion before scaling past $600/month.

1. Add Meta Pixel `Contact` event to every WhatsApp CTA button (fire server-side via CAPI)

### 1.1 Priority Persona Stack

## SECTION 3: FUNNEL ARCHITECTURE

**Funnel Architecture Score: 82/100**
*Recommended Funnel Type: WhatsApp-Integrated Purchase Funnel*
*Budget Split: TOFU 15% | MOFU 25% | BOFU 35% | Retargeting 25%*

### 3.1 Four-Stage Funnel

**STAGE 1 — TOFU (Awareness) — 15% of Budget**
- Objective: Brand awareness, video views, content engagement
- Audience: Cold traffic — broad interests (interior design, home decor, luxury lifestyle), age 25–50, Ghana
- Ad formats: Video ads (15–30s), Reels, TikTok creator content
- Platforms: Meta (video views campaign), TikTok (TopView or In-Feed)
- KPIs: CPM target <$2.50, video view rate >25%, thumb-stop ratio >20%
- Creative: "Before and After" transformation video, lifestyle room tours, identity hooks

**STAGE 2 — MOFU (Consideration) — 25% of Budget**
- Objective: Website traffic, WhatsApp message initiation, content consumption
- Audience: Warm — engaged with TOFU video (watched >50%), website visitors (last 30 days), Meta page followers
- Ad formats: Carousel (product showcase), single image (aspirational), Collection ads
- Platforms: Meta (Traffic + Engagement), Google Display (retargeting)
- KPIs: CPC <$0.40, CTR >1.5%, landing page session >45 seconds
- Creative: Room-specific carousels, persona-targeted copy ("For the home you've worked for"), WhatsApp CTA ("Ask us anything")

**STAGE 3 — BOFU (Conversion) — 35% of Budget**
- Objective: Website purchase or WhatsApp order initiation
- Audience: Hot — website product page visitors (last 14 days), WhatsApp initiators (not converted), cart abandoners
- Ad formats: Dynamic product ads (catalog), testimonial image with CTA, urgency/scarcity ads
- Platforms: Meta (Conversions campaign with Purchase or Contact event), Google Search (branded + high-intent keywords)
- KPIs: CPA target <$26 (break-even), ROAS target >3.8x, conversion rate >2.5%
- Creative: "The sofa you looked at is still available," customer testimonial stories, limited-quantity alerts

**STAGE 4 — Retargeting + Retention — 25% of Budget**
- Retargeting windows:
  - 1–3 days: Product viewed but not purchased → show same product + "Order via WhatsApp"
  - 3–7 days: Multiple products viewed → show curated "Complete the Look" bundle
  - 7–14 days: Cart abandoned → urgency CTA + free delivery reminder
  - 14–30 days: Homepage visited, no deeper engagement → lifestyle/inspiration content
  - 30–60 days: Past purchasers → new arrivals, "What's new at Clasikal"
- Cross-sell sequences: After bedroom purchase → show living room; after accent pieces → show furniture
- Frequency cap: 3 impressions/user/week on retargeting (avoid fatigue)

### 3.2 Conversion Path

```
Meta/TikTok Ad
    ↓
Product/Collection Landing Page
    ↓ (split path)
    ├── Website Checkout (fire Purchase event → CAPI mirror)
    └── WhatsApp CTA Click (fire Contact event → CAPI mirror)

            ↓
        Order Confirmed via Chat
            ↓
        Delivery Confirmation
            ↓
```

**Critical: Both paths must fire pixel events with matching `event_id` for CAPI deduplication.**

### 3.3 Pixel & CAPI Strategy

| Event | Trigger | Priority |
|-------|---------|---------|
| ViewContent | Product page load | High |
| AddToCart | Cart button click | High |
| InitiateCheckout | Checkout page load | High |
| **Contact** | **WhatsApp CTA click** | **Critical** |
| Purchase | Order confirmation page | High |
| Search | Site search | Medium |
| CompleteRegistration | Email signup | Medium |

**CAPI Configuration:** Mirror all 7 events server-side via Meta CAPI with `event_id` matching browser pixel. Target EMQ score ≥7.0 for Purchase and Contact events. Without this, up to 40% of WhatsApp-converted buyers are invisible to the ad algorithm.
